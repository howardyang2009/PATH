Privacy Policy

This is a courtesy translation. Only the German version is legally binding.

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data the hosted PATH application at path.rheinwegde.de (the Viewer, the Designer and their API) processes, why, and what rights you have. It does not cover a copy of PATH that you run yourself from the open-source code; that copy sends nothing to us.

1. Controller

Liying Zhu, RheinWeg
Im Rosengärtchen 70, 61440 Oberursel (Taunus), Germany

Email: rheinwegde@gmail.com

2. Your account and sign-in

You sign in through Clerk (Clerk, Inc., 660 King Street, Unit 345, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). Clerk stores your account data: email address, name and profile picture if you give them, the sign-in method you use, and session and security data such as IP address and browser type. PATH itself does not keep a user table. It receives only a signed token for each request and uses its user id (for example user_2abc…) to keep your files and runs apart from those of other users.

If you choose "Sign in with Google", Google (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) confirms your identity and shares your name, email address and profile picture with Clerk. PATH asks Google for no other data and has no access to your Google account, Gmail, Drive or contacts.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, because the account is needed to provide the service you asked for. Clerk processes the data on our behalf under a data processing agreement.

3. What you put into PATH

We store what you create and do in PATH so that the application works:

  • Workflows and step templates you save, private to you or shared with all users if you choose "shared". Other signed-in users can read a shared item. Only you can change or delete it.
  • Runs you launch: their inputs, outputs, logs, files and status. Runs are private to you.
  • Secrets you set on the Secrets page, such as API keys. They are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM). No page or API returns a secret value, and run logs mask them.
  • Usage counters used to enforce fair-use limits, such as run time per day.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Please do not put special categories of personal data (health, religion and similar) or other people's personal data into PATH unless you are allowed to.

4. Where the data is stored

PATH runs on our own server in Germany. Each run executes in its own isolated virtual machine on that server. Traffic reaches the server through Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA), which also manages DNS for rheinwegde.de. Cloudflare processes connection data, including your IP address, to deliver and protect the site.

Nightly backups are encrypted on our server before upload and stored with Backblaze (Backblaze, Inc., 201 Baldwin Ave, San Mateo, CA 94401, USA). Backblaze cannot read them.

Legal basis for hosting, security and backups: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is a secure, reliable service.

5. Third-party services your workflows call

A workflow step can call outside services, for example an AI model provider such as Anthropic or DeepSeek, or any web address a step names. When you run such a workflow, PATH sends the data that step uses to that service, with the credentials you supplied. That service then processes the data under its own terms and privacy policy. You decide which services your workflows call and what data they send.

6. Transfers outside the EU

Clerk, Google, Cloudflare and Backblaze may process data in the USA. Each of them is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, and their data processing agreements include the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Cookies and tracking

PATH uses only cookies and browser storage that are strictly necessary for the service you asked for (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG):

  • Clerk's session cookies (for example __session and __client_uat), which keep you signed in.
  • RHEINWEG_LOCALE, which stores the language you choose yourself in a language menu. It is set on .rheinwegde.de, so the choice also applies on the other RheinWeg pages, and it is kept for 1 year.
  • Browser storage for small interface settings.

We use no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

8. How long we keep data

  • Your workflows, templates, runs and secrets: until you delete them or your account.
  • Shared items that we remove for breaking the Terms: up to 30 days in quarantine, then deleted.
  • Backups: up to 6 months, after which deleted data is gone from every copy.
  • Account data at Clerk: until you delete your account. You can delete it from the account menu, or ask us by email; we then also delete your files, runs and secrets.

9. Your rights

Within the limits of the law, you have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • correct wrong data (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • restrict processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21 GDPR)

To use these rights, email the address above. You can also complain to a data protection authority. Ours is Der Hessische Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit, Gustav-Stresemann-Ring 1, 65189 Wiesbaden, Germany.

10. Children

PATH is not meant for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use data you already gave us, we tell you before it applies.
